# Proposed Helm Source-Available License v2.0

Status: REVIEW ONLY - NOT IN EFFECT. Prepared 2026-10-17.

This is draft legal language for owner and counsel review, not legal advice or
an operative grant. The root [LICENSE](../../../LICENSE) remains in effect.
Neither merging this document nor linking to it activates these terms. License
version 2.0 is a proposed legal-document identifier, not a Helm product version.

See the [proposal and clause comparison](../../legal/proposals/V020_LICENSING_POSITIONING.md)
for intent, conflicts and required approvals. Before activation, resolve every
bracketed placeholder, identify the exact covered artifacts/source revisions,
and separately authorize the governing-license and product-copy changes.

## Review Notes

The private source/contribution permissions, continuing version-specific grant
and termination cure are substantive proposals, not merely copy edits. They
need explicit approval alongside the business-use change. Paid terms, old-version
additional permissions and channel-specific agreements are not drafted here.
The [activation checklist](../../legal/proposals/V020_LICENSING_POSITIONING.md#small-v020-plan)
must be satisfied before extracting any of this text into a governing license.
